What we do

Three services,
one standard.

Whether you're selling a property, launching a product, or refining your visual direction — Khinna Visuals delivers images built to perform.

01

Real Estate Photography

Architecture, interiors, and atmosphere — captured with the precision that turns viewings into offers. We work with agents, developers, and private sellers across Luxembourg and the Greater Region.

  • Interior & exterior photography
  • Twilight & dusk sessions
  • Aerial & drone imagery
  • Virtual tour asset production
  • Same-day delivery available

02

Product Photography

Clean studio work or styled editorial — we photograph products that need to stand out on screen and shelf. From packshots to lifestyle campaigns, built for e-commerce and brand communications.

  • Clean-background packshots
  • Lifestyle & in-context shots
  • 360° product spin sets
  • Food & beverage photography
  • White-label retouching

03

Visual Consulting

Not sure where your visuals are letting you down? We audit your existing image library, define a brief, and create a shoot plan that aligns every frame with your brand objectives.

  • Visual identity audit
  • Shot list & brief creation
  • Art direction for shoots
  • Ongoing retainer available
  • Agency & studio partnerships

About

Photography is
observation.

Khinna Visuals is a Luxembourg-based photography studio specialising in real estate and product imagery. Founded on the belief that every image should have a reason to exist — and a job to do — we bring a methodical, light-led approach to every commission.

We work closely with estate agencies, property developers, e-commerce brands, and creative agencies across Luxembourg, the Greater Region, and beyond — building long-term visual libraries as well as one-off campaigns.
